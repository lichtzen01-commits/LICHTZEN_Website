HOW WE WORK

A technical partner who reviews specifications with you.

Whether you are installing new equipment or replacing a lamp in use, we start with the information you need.

  1. 01

    Check process conditions

    We check the material, curing target, production conditions and installation space.

  2. 02

    Review light source and specifications

    We review wavelength, irradiation area, cooling and transport conditions together.

  3. 03

    Design & manufacturing

    We build to the equipment configuration and mechanical and control conditions.

  4. 04

    Replacement & maintenance

    We provide light sources, parts and after-sales service during operation.
