WHY LICHTZEN?

A light source maker
that designs systems and lines.

Because we make our own UV lamps and LED modules, you don't need several suppliers for the light source, equipment and production line that fit your process. LICHTZEN reviews it all at once and takes responsibility to the end.

SINCE 1995

31 years of light technology, changing our customers' productivity

Starting with UV systems in 1995, we have expanded into UV LED curing, IR drying and UV-C disinfection.

IN-HOUSE LIGHT SOURCES

We make it ourselves, from the source of the light

Mercury, metal halide and gallium UV lamps, MIR·NIR lamps and UV·IR LEDs are produced in our Gunpo factory.

CUSTOM & SAMPLE TEST

Beyond the original, a better alternative

We build replacements for discontinued or imported lamps in small quantities and confirm curing conditions with sample tests first.

OPTICAL & THERMAL DESIGN

Designing light performance with data, not guesswork

Optical simulation, thermal and airflow analysis and 22 light measurement instruments shape uniformity and lifetime.

LINE INTEGRATION

From a single machine to the entire production line

A 15 m assembly line and our affiliate Ohsung Unicon's conveyors and 40 m line assembly let us take on whole production lines.

QUALITY & GLOBAL

Our technology at work in industries across 39 countries

ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certified, 18 registered patents and supply experience in 39 countries.

ALL-IN-ONE SOLUTION

From light source to production line,
solved in one place.

Lamps, LEDs, curing and drying equipment and entire production lines: no need for several suppliers. With LICHTZEN alone, you go from consultation to design and manufacturing.

  1. 01
    STEP 01

    Process analysis

    We check materials, line speed, target temperature and installation space to define what you need.

  2. 02
    STEP 02

    Light source selection & testing

    We run curing and drying tests on your own material with candidate lamps and LEDs.

  3. 03
    STEP 03

    Equipment & line design

    We design the optics, thermal, mechanics and controls, and finalize the line layout including conveyors.

  4. 04
    STEP 04

    Manufacturing & quality verification

    Assembled in our own factory, then shipped after irradiance and temperature are measured.

  5. 05
    STEP 05

    Delivery & installation

    We agree on installation conditions in advance and show you how to operate it after delivery.

  6. 06
    STEP 06

    Operation & maintenance

    Replacement lamps, parts and service keep supporting you after start-up.
