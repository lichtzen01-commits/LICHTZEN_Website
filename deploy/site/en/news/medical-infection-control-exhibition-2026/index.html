Exhibiting at the 31st Annual Conference of the Korean Society for Healthcare-associated Infection Control and Prevention (28–29 May 2026)

LICHTZEN news & product materials

LICHTZEN Co., Ltd.2026.05.27


LICHTZEN Co., Ltd. (CEO Sung Soo Hyun) is exhibiting on Thursday 28 and Friday 29 May 2026 at the Swiss Grand Hotel,

at the exhibition held alongside the 31st Annual Conference and Training of the Korean Society for Healthcare-associated Infection Control and Prevention, presenting its "UV-C disinfection solutions", 

including the following UV-C based infection control solutions.

· 254 nm unmanned disinfection systems
  - UV-C automated disinfection unit for healthcare settings (standard)
  - UV-C automated disinfection unit for healthcare settings (compact)
· 222 nm disinfection systems for occupied spaces
  - Far-UVC(Occupied Space UV-C Disinfection Solution)

The UV-C automated disinfection unit for healthcare settings was co-developed with an infection control institution, supported by the Korea Pandemic Preparedness and Response Foundation (GFID),

through a national R&D project (Ministry of Health and Welfare, project no. RS-2025-02633901).

The 222 nm Far-UVC solution delivers disinfection comparable to conventional 254 nm UV-C lamps,

and is a next-generation technology that allows continuous disinfection of air and surfaces even where people are present.

The UV-C (254 nm) automated disinfection unit can be used in hospital ICUs, for disinfecting general wards after discharge, and for disinfecting unoccupied operating rooms

after surgery, helping prevent infections and improve environmental management in healthcare facilities.

The Far-UVC (222 nm) solution can be used in hospitals and healthcare facilities as well as public transport, vehicle interiors, schools, offices, cafés and food and beverage businesses

and other spaces where people are present, and can be extended to livestock housing as a disinfection solution against animal infectious diseases.

Building on the solutions shown at this exhibition, LICHTZEN will continue to use next-generation UV-C infection control technology

to help create safe and sustainable disinfection environments.

LICHTZEN (CEO: Sung Soo-hyun) will participate in the exhibition held alongside the 31st Annual Conference and Training Program

of the Korean Society for Healthcare-associated Infection Control and Prevention,

taking place from May 28 (Thu) to May 29 (Fri), 2026, at Swiss Grand Hotel. During the exhibition,

the company will showcase its “UV-C Disinfection Solutions” and present the following UV-C-based infection control solutions.

· 254nm Unoccupied UV-C Disinfection Systems

  -UV-C Automated Disinfection System for Medical Environments (Standard Model)
  -UV-C Automated Disinfection System for Medical Environments (Compact Model)

· 222nm Occupied-Space UV-C Disinfection System

  -Far-UVC (Occupied Space UV-C Disinfection Solution)

In particular, the UV-C Automated Disinfection System for Medical Environments was jointly developed with infection control specialists

under the support of the Government-wide Foundation for Infectious Disease Research and Development (GFID),

through a national R&D project funded by the Ministry of Health and Welfare.(Project No. RS-2025-02633901)

The 222nm Far-UVC solution provides sterilization performance equivalent to that of conventional 254nm UV-C lamps

while enabling continuous disinfection of air and surfaces even in occupied spaces, representing a next-generation disinfection technology.

The UV-C (254nm) Automated Disinfection System for Medical Environments can be applied to intensive care units (ICUs),

environmental disinfection of patient rooms after discharge, and operating rooms in unoccupied conditions after surgery,

thereby contributing to infection prevention and improved environmental hygiene management within healthcare facilities.

In addition, the Far-UVC (222nm) solution can be utilized in a wide range of occupied environments,

including hospitals and healthcare facilities, public transportation, vehicle interiors, schools, offices, cafés,

and food & beverage workplaces.

Furthermore, it can also be expanded for use in livestock facilities as a disinfection solution for the prevention of animal infectious diseases.

Based on the solutions to be showcased at this exhibition,

LICHTZEN will continue to contribute to the establishment of safer and more sustainable disinfection environments through next-generation

UV-C-based infection control technologies.
