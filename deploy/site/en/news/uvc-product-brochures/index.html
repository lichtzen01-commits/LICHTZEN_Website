The materials attached to this post are introductions to LICHTZEN's disinfection products using UV-C 222 nm and 254 nm wavelengths.
Based on the characteristics of each wavelength and the disinfection principles that use them,
you can see LICHTZEN's UV disinfection technology, which considers both human safety and germicidal performance.

The devices are designed for a range of settings such as healthcare facilities and public buildings,
and provide reliable disinfection solutions built on LICHTZEN's accumulated UV technology.
