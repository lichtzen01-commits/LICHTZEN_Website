Privacy policy

Article 1 Purposes of processing personal information

The Company processes personal information for the following purposes. Personal information is not used for any other purpose; if the purpose changes, the Company takes necessary measures such as obtaining separate consent under Article 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act.

  1. Responding to inquiries and consultations: receiving product, quotation and technical inquiries, sample test, lamp replacement and after-sales requests; identity verification; replies and results
  2. Quotations and contract performance: providing quotations and specifications, concluding and performing contracts, and contacting for delivery, installation and after-sales support
  3. Handling complaints: confirming complaints, contacting for fact-finding, and notifying results

Article 3 Processing and retention period

The Company processes and retains personal information within the retention period required by law or consented to by the data subject.

  1. Inquiries and consultations: after the inquiry has been handled, [To be confirmed before launch: e.g. 3 years]. However, if it leads to a quotation or contract, the statutory retention periods below apply.
  2. Records related to quotations and contracts: retention periods under relevant laws
    • Records on contracts or withdrawal of offers: 5 years (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
    • Records on payment and supply of goods: 5 years (same Act)
    • Records on consumer complaints or dispute resolution: 3 years (same Act)
    • Transaction records such as tax invoices: 5 years (Framework Act on National Taxes, Corporate Tax Act)
    • Website access logs: 3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act)

Article 4 Provision to third parties

The Company processes personal information only within the purposes in Article 1 and provides it to third parties only in the cases set out in Articles 17 and 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act, such as with the data subject's consent or where specifically required by law. No personal information is currently provided to third parties.

When outsourcing, it specifies in writing, in accordance with Article 26 of the Personal Information Protection Act, a prohibition on processing beyond the outsourced work, technical and administrative safeguards, restrictions on re-outsourcing, supervision of the processor and liability for damages, and supervises whether the processor handles personal information securely.

Article 7 Destruction procedures and methods

  1. Procedure: Personal information whose retention period has expired or whose purpose has been achieved is destroyed without delay (within 5 days). Where it must be kept under law, it is stored separately from other personal information.
  2. Method: Electronic files are deleted using technical methods that prevent recovery, and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.

Article 8 Rights and obligations of data subjects and legal representatives and how to exercise them

  1. Data subjects may at any time request access to, correction or deletion of, suspension of processing of their personal information, or withdraw consent.
  2. Rights may be exercised in writing, by phone or by email, and the Company will act without delay (within 10 days).
  3. Rights may also be exercised through a legal representative or an authorized person, in which case a power of attorney must be submitted.
  4. Deletion cannot be requested for personal information that laws require to be collected, and requests for access or suspension may be limited where other laws specifically provide.
  5. The Company verifies that the person making a request is the data subject or a legitimate representative.

The Company does not collect personal information of children under 14.

Article 9 Measures to ensure the security of personal information

  1. Administrative measures: establishing and implementing an internal management plan, minimizing and regularly training staff who handle personal information
  2. Technical measures: access rights management and access control for processing systems, encryption in transit (HTTPS), installing and updating security software, and keeping access logs
  3. Physical measures: keeping documents and storage media under lock and controlling access to storage areas

Article 10 Installation, operation and refusal of automatic collection devices

The Company's website does not use cookies to identify users or for advertising. Display settings such as the selected language are stored only in the user's browser and are not sent to the Company. Users can delete stored information or refuse storage at any time in their browser settings. If a visitor analytics tool is introduced, its details and how to refuse will be disclosed in this policy.
