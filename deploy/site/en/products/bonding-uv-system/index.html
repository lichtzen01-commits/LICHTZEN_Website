Features

  • Coating and printing on PET film, glass, paper and PVD thin films
  • Better UV curing quality with nitrogen (N₂) purging
  • Blocks oxygen from outside (prevents surface oxidation)
  • Special quartz window
  • Prevents contamination of lamp and reflector, extending life and avoiding UV energy loss
  • Low nitrogen consumption reduces cost
  • Sliding lamp housing for easy lamp replacement
  • Cold mirror & hot mirror
  • Works with both mercury and metal halide lamps (3–16 kW)
  • Forced air cooling
  • Curing width: 200–1,500 mm
