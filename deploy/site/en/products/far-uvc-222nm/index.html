SAFETY BASIS

A wavelength absorbed in the outer skin

222 nm is mostly absorbed in the dead outer layer of the epidermis, so very little reaches living basal cells or the eyes.

222nm Far-UVCAbsorbed in outer layer

Mostly absorbed in the dead outer layer of the skin, so it barely reaches living cells. It can be used with people present.

254nm UV-CReaches living cells

Acts strongly on microbial DNA and disinfects fast, but it reaches living human cells, so it is used only in unoccupied rooms.

FAQ

Can Far-UVC 222 nm be used while people are present?

222 nm is mostly absorbed in the outer dead-cell layer of the skin, so its skin TLV is 479 mJ/cm², far above 254 nm (10 mJ/cm²). Within ACGIH limits it can run continuously in occupied rooms, provided each model's recommended installation distance and irradiation range are followed.

How do Types A, B and C differ?

Type A is for small spaces (up to 6 W), Type B for indoor rooms (up to 12 W) and Type C for large spaces (up to 30 W). Choosing the model that matches the room gives more effective disinfection.

How was the efficacy verified?

In Korea Conformity Laboratories (KCL) tests at 30 cm for 13 minutes, E. coli and Salmonella (S. enterica) were each reduced by 99.9%.

How long do the lamps last?

Lamps are warranted for 5,000 hours and can last up to about 8,000 hours depending on conditions. The unit has a 1-year free service warranty.
