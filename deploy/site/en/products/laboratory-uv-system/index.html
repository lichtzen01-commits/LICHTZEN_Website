Features

  • Benchtop unit, suitable as a lab curing unit
  • Accepts medium/high-pressure UV lamps (mercury, metal halide, ozone-free)
  • 220VAC, 2P, 50/60Hz
  • Band-pass filter mount (option)
  • Speed meter and lamp hour timer
  • Forced air cooling
  • Aluminium reflector, high-brightness aluminium reflector, cold mirror, hot mirror (options)
  • 750(L) × 340(D) × 880(H)mm
