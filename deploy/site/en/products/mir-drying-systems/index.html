High-efficiency MIR drying technology

  • Electromagnetic waves in the 2.0–6.0 μm wavelength range, called medium-wave infrared (MIR).
  • Thermal radiation is the emission of electromagnetic waves when groups of atoms in a material are excited by heat; it is also called temperature radiation.
  • MIR energy has the wavelength band best suited to drying coated metal and non-metal products, and is particularly efficient for drying water-based paints, polymer resins (plastics) and glass.
  • Similar to conventional IR drying systems, it covers the part of the IR emission that actually affects drying most.

Electromagnetic radiation spectrum (MIR range: about 2–6 μm)

Advantages of MIR drying systems

  • Easy to apply and install, consisting of a compact MIR irradiation system and power supply.
  • Standardized modules in various structures and capacities make it easy to build application systems.
  • Even without background in MIR drying — in printing equipment, coating equipment or FPD production lines — modular systems make it simple to configure an MIR drying system.
  • Only the modules and power supplies you need are used, reducing equipment cost.
  • Supports various input/output signals for easy interfacing with peripheral equipment.
