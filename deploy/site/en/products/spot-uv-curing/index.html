Features

  • Suited to applications needing high UV intensity in specific areas
  • Light-guide irradiation concentrates UV intensity on the target area, shortening curing time
  • Fast curing of thick adhesives and pressure-sensitive adhesives
  • Microprocessor control
  • PLC interface
  • High brightness / high power factor
  • Compact design
  • UV lamp can be switched on/off by the user
  • Remembers operating mode and resumes automatically
  • Shutter on/off by foot switch
  • MANUAL / AUTO / PULSE modes
  • LCD display
  • Easy lamp replacement
  • Economical, reasonable price
  • CE certified

Applications

  • Chip bonding, bonding optical parts, spot curing of screen printing
  • Fixing electronic components, bonding glass parts (ceramics, lenses, etc.)
  • Finishing coil windings (bonding), adhesive seals for liquid crystals
  • Protective coatings for electrical materials (potting & encapsulating)
  • Bonding heat-sensitive materials and medical device parts
  • Other applications needing spot curing
