UV light has countless applications across all industries beyond those listed below. Using UV coatings reduces the harmful gases released during drying and curing, protecting operators' health and reducing environmental pollution.

Surfaces cured with UV are also very hard and can be used for many purposes.
The biggest advantage is that products dry and cure within 1–2 seconds, dramatically raising productivity.
