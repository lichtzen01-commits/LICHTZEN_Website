HOW TO USE

How is it used?

  1. STEP 1

    Position the unit

    Move Wonder-Ray® into the room to be disinfected and position it.

  2. STEP 2

    Set up disinfection your way

    Set the disinfection time from the touchscreen, remote, phone or tablet.

  3. STEP 3

    Start disinfection

    Check the settings, then start UV-C disinfection.

  4. STEP 4

    Disinfection is complete

    When the set time ends, disinfection is complete and the room can be used again.

* The exact disinfection time depends on room size and conditions of use.

KEY FEATURES

Wonder-Ray® key features

A UV-C disinfection system for healthcare, designed for safety and ease of use

  • 360° motion detectionA safety function that cuts lamp power when a person is detected during UV exposure.
  • Wing-type upper lampsIrradiates upward as well, reducing blind spots in the room.
  • Right dose for the roomUV-C exposure conditions are set for the room's size and layout.
  • Built for moving and storageAutomatic raising and lowering and a folding design make it easy to move and store.
  • Shatter-resistant designA film reduces scattering of glass fragments if a lamp breaks.
  • Easy-move wheelsWheels roll easily over small steps and door sills, so moving between rooms is simple.
SAFETY

Safety designed around real use

  • Human-presence sensorPower is cut automatically if someone approaches during UV exposure.
  • Emergency stopThe unit can be stopped immediately if something unexpected happens.
  • Status lightWhite (standby) · blinking blue (ready to disinfect) · steady blue (disinfecting) · steady red (error, full stop) · blinking red (error alarm, restarts after a set time).
  • Ozone suppressionBlocks the wavelengths that can produce ozone, keeping indoor ozone below the 0.06 ppm limit after disinfection.
  • KC certificationKC-registered in Korea, meeting safety and conformity requirements.
  • Rules of useOperate only in unoccupied rooms, and do not look at the unit or expose your body while it runs.

FAQ

Can the Wonder-Ray® 254 nm disinfector be used with people present?

No. 254 nm UV-C also strongly affects people, so it must run only in unoccupied rooms. The 360° motion sensor cuts lamp power immediately if a person or animal is detected.

How do I choose between the standard (S8W4) and compact (S8) models?

The standard model disinfects the whole room with 12 lamps, including the wing-type upper lamps, with 99.999%+ efficacy confirmed at 2 m. The compact model uses four 105 cm lamps with 99.99%+ efficacy at 1 m and suits smaller rooms.

How is the disinfection time set?

The right exposure time is calculated by simulation for the room's size and layout; 30 minutes or more is recommended. It is set from the touch panel or remote, or optionally from a tablet or smartphone.

What are the lamp life and warranty?

Lamps are warranted for 5,000 hours and cumulative hours are shown on the unit. The unit has a 1-year free service warranty.
