FAQ

What information is needed for a custom equipment consultation?

Tell us the material, curing/drying target, irradiation width, transport conditions and installation space. Attaching equipment photos, drawings or specifications helps a detailed review.

  • Material
  • What to cure or dry
  • Irradiation width
  • Conveying conditions
  • Installation space
  • Photos & drawings
Can I also ask about replacing existing lamps or parts?

Yes. Send the existing model name, dimensions, and photos of the equipment and light source in use. We will check product specifications and application conditions with you.

  • Current model name
  • Specs & dimensions
  • Equipment photo
  • Light source photo
Should I choose a UV lamp or UV LED?

It depends on the curing wavelength of your ink, adhesive or coating, how heat-sensitive the material is, line speed and the area to cure. Tell us your materials and process conditions and we will compare both and suggest the right source.

UV lampsBroad spectrumWide, high-output processes
UV LEDSpecific wavelengthHeat-sensitive materials
Can you test curing or drying on our samples first?

Send us your material samples and the result you need (degree of cure, surface finish, temperature limits). We will check whether a test is possible and explain how it would run.

  1. Send samples
  2. Review conditions
  3. Test plan
Can the equipment be added to our existing line?

Share the conveyor width and speed, the space available, power and cooling conditions and photos of the site. We will review a configuration that fits your current line.

  • Conveyor width & speed
  • Installation space
  • Power & cooling
  • Site photos
