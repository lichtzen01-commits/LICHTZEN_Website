What to check before reviewing equipment

What information is needed for a custom equipment consultation?

Tell us the material, curing/drying target, irradiation width, transport conditions and installation space. Attaching equipment photos, drawings or specifications helps a detailed review.

Can I also ask about replacing existing lamps or parts?

Yes. Send the existing model name, dimensions, and photos of the equipment and light source in use. We will check product specifications and application conditions with you.
