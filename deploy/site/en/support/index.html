Frequently checked before a consultation

Can I ask about a replacement lamp even if I don't know its specifications?

Please prepare photos of the lamp and base, its overall length, the equipment it is used in, and any model name you can find. Our staff will tell you what else to check.

What is needed to review custom equipment?

Please prepare the material, irradiation width, transport conditions, installation space, and photos or drawings of the equipment.

How do I request drawings not in the catalogue?

Enter the product name and the materials you need in the inquiry form. Our staff will confirm what can be disclosed or provided.
